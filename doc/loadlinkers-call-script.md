# Loadlinkers — Broker Cold Call Script

**Goal of every call:** get permission to text the trial link, or book a 15-minute demo.
**Backup goal:** a free Marketplace signup.
**Rule:** only text after the prospect says "sure" / "okay". Log the date permission was given.

---

## 1. OPENER (every call)

> "Hey [Name]? It's [First Name], [Full Name]. I'm looking at your FMCSA profile here, I saw your authority went active recently, and I was wondering if you could help me out for a moment?"

**Them:** "Sure, what's this about?"

> "Well, I'm not even sure if it makes sense for us to talk yet. I'm calling new brokers to see who's still running loads and rate cons on spreadsheets, because that usually costs hours every week and causes billing mistakes. Is that something you're dealing with?"

→ Excel / interested: go to **2. YES PATH**
→ Objection: go to **3. NO PATH**

---

## 2. YES PATH

**Them:** "Yeah, we're on Excel."

> "Got it. How many loads are you running a week, roughly?"

*(Listen. Note the number in CRM.)*

> "Okay. We built Loadlinkers TMS for exactly that stage. Rate cons, FMCSA carrier checks and invoices in one place, and we'll import your spreadsheet for you. There's a 7-day free trial. Would you be opposed to me texting you the trial link so you can try a load or two on your own time?"

**Them:** "Sure."

> "Perfect. Is this the best number to text?"

> "And would you be against a quick 15-minute call on [day] at [time], so I can import a few of your loads live and show you around?"

**Close:**
> "Great, I'll text you the link right now. Talk to you [day]."

---

## 3. NO PATH (objections)

**"We already have a TMS."**
> "Oh nice, which one are you using? … And if you could change one thing about it, what would it be?"
*(Pick up on their pain point.)*
> "That's actually one of the reasons we built ours. Would you be opposed to me texting you the trial link, just to compare?"

**"We use Ascend, it's free."**
> "Ascend is solid. Ours is built to be simpler, and we import your data for free. Would you be against taking 15 minutes to compare side by side?"

**"Not interested."**
> "Totally fair. Just so I know, is it because you're set with a system, or it's just not the right time?"
*(If "not the right time")* → "Would it be okay if I check back in a month?"

**"Send me an email."**
> "Happy to. What should I put in it so it's actually useful for you, pricing or a quick video? … And would you be opposed to a quick look on [day], so it's not just another email in your inbox?"

**"How much is it?"**
> "It starts at $69 a month, and there's a 7-day free trial, so you can try it with real loads before paying anything."

**"I'm busy right now."**
> "No problem. When's a better time, later today or tomorrow morning?"
*(Get an exact time before hanging up.)*

**If they still say no to the TMS → go to 4. MARKETPLACE BACKUP**

---

## 4. MARKETPLACE BACKUP (after TMS is rejected)

> "Totally understand. One more thing before I let you go, and this one's free. We also run the Loadlinkers Marketplace, where we personally connect brokers with freight agents and dispatchers. You post what you're looking for, and when we find a good fit, we introduce you. Would you be opposed to me texting you the link to create a free profile?"

**"How does it work?"**
> "You sign up with what you need, like an agent with their own shippers, or a dispatcher. Profiles stay private until we make a match, then we introduce both sides. No cost to join."

**Final no:**
> "Understood, thanks for your time [Name]. Have a good one."

---

## 5. MARKETPLACE-FIRST OPENER

Use instead of the normal opener when CRM notes show the broker is looking for agents (job post, Facebook post, etc.).

> "Hey [Name]? It's [First Name], [Full Name]. I'm looking at your FMCSA profile here, and I was wondering if you could help me out for a moment?"

> "Well, I'm not even sure if it makes sense for us to talk yet. I'm calling new brokers to see who's looking to bring on freight agents, because finding good agents is usually the hardest part of the first year. Is that something you're working on?"

→ Then pitch the Marketplace (section 4), and mention the TMS trial at the end.

## 9. CRM CALL OUTCOMES

| Outcome | Next action |
|---|---|
| Demo booked | Send calendar invite + reminder text 1 hour before |
| Trial link sent | Follow-up call on day 2 and day 6 of trial |
| Marketplace link sent | Check signup in 2 days |
| Callback requested | Call at the exact time agreed |
| Email requested | Send email same day, call again in 3 days |
| Voicemail left | Try again in 2 days (max 3 voicemails) |
| Not interested | Retry in 60 days |
| Do not call | Never call or text again |
| Wrong number | Remove number |

---

## Notes for callers
- Never claim data we don't have (their lanes, load postings, etc.). Only FMCSA profile info is real.
- Don't call the Marketplace a "load board" — it gives introductions, not loads.
- Don't make claims about how many agents/dispatchers are on the Marketplace.
- Best calling times (US Eastern): 10–11:30am and 2–4pm. Avoid lunch.
