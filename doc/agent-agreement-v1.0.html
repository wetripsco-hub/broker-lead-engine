Sales Agent Code of Conduct & Commission Agreement

1. Parties and relationship

This Agreement is between Loadlinker Logistics LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Loadlinkers" or the "Company"), and the individual who signs below (the "Agent").

The Agent is an independent contractor who promotes the Company's products, including Loadlinkers TMS and related services. The Agent is not an employee, partner, joint venturer or legal representative of the Company, and has no authority to sign contracts, make commitments or incur obligations on the Company's behalf. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

2. Compensation

  1. The Agent is paid by commission only. No salary, base pay, hourly pay, benefits, allowance, reimbursement or guaranteed income is offered or implied.
  2. Commission is 30% of the net subscription revenue the Company actually receives from each customer the Agent brings, including extra user seats.
  3. Commission is recurring for as long as the customer stays subscribed and pays. A sale counts only after the customer's payment is received by the Company.
  4. Commissions are paid on the 15th of each month for the previous month's sales, with a written statement.
  5. Commission for a customer stops in the month that customer cancels or stops paying. All commission stops when the Agent leaves the program or this Agreement ends, unless the Company agrees otherwise in writing.
  6. The Company may adjust commission for refunds, chargebacks, failed payments, discounts or fraud.
  7. The Agent is solely responsible for all taxes, fees and filings on income received under this Agreement in the Agent's own country.
  8. Any different commission terms apply only if the Company offers them to the Agent individually in writing or in the Agent's portal.

3. Conduct and selling rules

  1. Sell honestly. Use only official pricing, plans and Company-approved materials.
  2. Make no false, misleading or unauthorized promises about the product, pricing, results, discounts or contract terms.
  3. Never collect payments from customers or accept money on the Company's behalf.
  4. Contact only the leads assigned to you in the Company portal.
  5. Follow all calling rules, including calling hours, call limits, cooldowns and the call-recording disclosure.
  6. Send text messages only after the prospect agrees.
  7. Do not use the Company's name, logo or materials in any way that is not approved in writing.
  8. Comply with all applicable laws, including telemarketing, consumer protection, anti-spam and data protection laws.

4. Loadlinkers TMS, portal and data

The Agent receives access to the Loadlinkers sales portal and may see information about Loadlinkers TMS, its customers and its leads. The Agent must not misuse any Loadlinkers TMS or portal data. In particular, the Agent must not:

  1. access any data, account or feature beyond what the Company has authorized;
  2. copy, download, export, screenshot for sharing, scrape or record leads, customer details, call recordings or any other portal or TMS data;
  3. share login credentials, or let any other person use the Agent's account;
  4. use any Loadlinkers data for personal purposes, for another business, or for anything other than the Agent's assigned work;
  5. sell, rent, publish or disclose Loadlinkers data to any third party;
  6. reverse engineer, copy or attempt to replicate Loadlinkers TMS or the portal;
  7. enter false information, alter records, or try to bypass security, call limits or other controls.

The Agent must report any loss of credentials, unauthorized access or data incident to the Company immediately. All Loadlinkers data and systems remain the exclusive property of the Company. Any misuse is a material breach: the Company may end this Agreement immediately, withhold commission connected to the misuse, and take legal action.

5. Confidentiality

The Agent must keep confidential all non-public information about the Company, including pricing, systems, leads, customers, scripts, materials and agent terms. This duty continues after this Agreement ends. On request or when this Agreement ends, the Agent must delete or return any Company information in the Agent's possession.

6. Indemnity

The Agent will indemnify and hold the Company harmless from any claim, loss, fine, penalty or cost, including reasonable legal fees, that results from the Agent's breach of this Agreement, false or unauthorized statements, unlawful conduct, or misuse of data.

7. Non-solicitation

During this Agreement and for 12 months after it ends, the Agent will not use Company leads or customer information to solicit those leads or customers for any competing product or service.

8. Term and termination

This Agreement starts when the Agent signs and continues until either party ends it by written notice, including email. The Company may end it immediately for breach, fraud, misrepresentation or data misuse. Commission already earned and payable under Section 2 before termination will be paid, except where Section 4 allows it to be withheld.

9. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, the Company is not liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost income, other than commission properly owed under this Agreement.

10. Governing law and dispute resolution

  1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, United States, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.
  2. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement will be resolved by final and binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, before a single arbitrator. The arbitration will be conducted online, by video conference and written submissions, in English. The legal seat of arbitration is Wyoming, United States. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court with jurisdiction.
  3. Disputes will be resolved on an individual basis only, not as part of any class, group or representative proceeding.
  4. The Company may still ask any competent court for urgent or injunctive relief to protect its confidential information, data or intellectual property.

11. General

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and replaces any earlier discussion. The Company may publish updated versions; an updated version applies to the Agent only after the Agent accepts it. If any part of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. The Agent's typed name and confirmation below are an electronic signature with the same effect as a handwritten signature.
